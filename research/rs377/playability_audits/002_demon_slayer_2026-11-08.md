## Confirmed source defects and changes

Patch `content-patches/rs377/patches/0032-demon-slayer-keys-and-boss-gates.patch` (number may be re-sequenced if a parallel PR changed the patch chain) includes four text files:

1. **Rovin duplicate key:** A player could repeat the three-dialogue-options path to receive unlimited key-2 items. Now checks inventory and bank and refuses if already held; refuses a full pack before handing over key.
2. **Pre-quest drain:** A water bucket used on the drain prior to Sir Prysin's key hunt could create a sewer key. Now refuses early and preserves the bucket.
3. **Silverlight inventory safety:** The free pre-boss replacement could claim to give the sword into a full inventory; the 500-coin replacement could take coins without room to receive it. Now checks capacity, still permitting an exact 500-coin stack to free its own inventory slot.
4. **Boss stage gate:** Previously the two NPC combat procedures checked only completion and whether Silverlight was equipped. Now both reject Delrith combat until stage 29, preventing a stray Silverlight from bypassing quest setup.

No item definitions, dialogues, post-2006 content, player saves, or client binaries have been replaced.

## Full acceptance sequence still required

Use an isolated disposable revision-377 account/world, with provenance and build identifiers; do not use the owner's or children's saves as the test fixture.

1. Talk to Aris without coin and then with one coin; test refuse/accept, three dialogue routes and incantation reminder. Confirm stage 0→1, no progress or coins deducted before acceptance.
2. Talk to Prysin at level 0; trigger stage 2 key hunt; reach Rovin upstairs by normal ladders/stairs. Obtain one key, repeat dialogue (no duplicate), bank key and repeat (no duplicate), lose it and retrieve legitimately.
3. Get palace empty bucket, fill at kitchen sink, use on drain. Verify pre-hunt bucket is not wasted and cannot spawn a key; after stage 2 verify one private sewer key appears in the sewer and is visible only to that player.
4. Open manhole, traverse collision/path to key, pick it up in inventory and confirm simultaneous second account cannot loot it.
5. Meet Traiborn in Wizards' Tower; offer bones in batches, including full inventory; confirm all 25 remove exactly once, stage 3→28 and one key; relog mid-way and resume without reset.
6. Trade precisely the three keys for Silverlight (stage 29), verify inventory/coin conservation and free replacement before completion only if previously lost. Reject full-inventory sword grant and 500-coin payment unless the slot will free.
7. Fight Delrith using Silverlight, verify blocked attacks before correct stage and without equipped sword, actual HP/damage, weaken trigger, wrong incantation reset and correct incantation banishment.
8. Confirm quest stage 30, completion interface, exactly 3 QP, no duplicate reward, no further Delrith battle. Log out and relog and confirm persistent quest, XP, quest points, inventory, bank and spent bones.
