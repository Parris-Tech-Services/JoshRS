WHY THE EXTERNAL LINK?

Why isn't the game inside JoshRS yet?

Lost City's pages explicitly prevent iframe embedding. Running a fully independent game at this domain instead requires deploying the real TypeScript server, compatible client, content, a database, persistent character saves, HTTPS and WebSockets. We must also respect Jagex's separate ownership of historical assets.

We've verified that embedding fails; we won't create a credential-intercepting proxy or pretend a redirect is our own hosted world.
