## 2. The three decisions blocking further work

These are **yours to make**, not an agent's. Nothing below them can be scheduled until they are.

### (a) Which revision is the family target?

The browser world is **274** (May 2004). The bots and Elrothiro play **377**. The owners' sheet has
88 quests; 22 are absent from 274 for era reasons. Options: port content forward into 274, or move
the playable target to a later revision. Auditing cannot resolve this.

### (b) Is the native client still the goal?

It needs a 3D renderer, models, animations, interfaces, audio, movement, combat, inventory,
banking, trading and chat. That is months. Alternatively the browser world already satisfies
"playable on every platform" and needs only testing, not building.

### (c) How much invented content is acceptable for Sylvie?

The ADR forbids passing invented dialogue off as authentic, and permits clearly-labelled family-mode
extras. A companion NPC is permitted **as a labelled original**, not as RuneScape content. This
matters because the natural next step ("make Lumbridge friendlier") is exactly where authenticity
and family-friendly pull in opposite directions.

## 7. Traps that cost real time this session

- **`obj.dat` has no offset table** — count then definitions back to back. An offset-table reader
  produces non-monotonic garbage.
- **`npc.dat` opcode 106 is a multivarbit block**, not a `u16`. Measure the engine; do not transcribe
  its source. `NpcType.prototype.decode.toString()` disagreed with the file on disk.
- **Rewards live in the queue block *or* immediately before `send_quest_complete.**
- **Never `head -N` a reward extraction.** It sorts `inv_add` ahead of `stat_advance`.
- **Never trust a directory name.** `quest_itgronigen` = Observatory, `quest_desertrescue` = Tourist
  Trap, `quest_troll` = Troll Stronghold, `quest_prince` = Prince Ali.
- **`Jagfile377` is both a route enum and a container reader** if you are careless; they were renamed
  to `CacheJagfile377` and `Jagfile377` respectively.
- **The OSRS wiki is a modern superset.** Using its reward values on 274 content adds inauthentic
  content; seven such rewards were confirmed modern and correctly absent.
