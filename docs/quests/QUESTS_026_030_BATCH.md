## Temple of Ikov — both pendants is correct

Content grants **both** the Pendant of Lucien and the Armadyl pendant. That looks like
double-rewarding, but it matches the quest: Lucien's pendant is given early, and the Armadyl
pendant comes later if you side with Armadyl. The wiki lists them separately for exactly that
reason.

## One era question

**Clock Tower** — OSRS lists 500 coins **and 1,000 Firemaking XP**; content grants the coins
only. Recorded as **unresolved**, not changed.

That makes four unresolved era questions now (Pirate's Treasure, Lost City, Merlin's Crystal,
Clock Tower), all of the same shape: OSRS adds XP to a quest that content treats as
reward-only. They will all resolve together once a 2004-era source is checked, so they are
tracked as a single open question rather than four separate investigations.
