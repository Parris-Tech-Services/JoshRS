## Two unresolved, not "gaps"

**Pirate's Treasure** — the OSRS page lists 800 Cooking XP; content grants 450 coins, a gold
ring, an emerald and no XP. **Lost City** — OSRS lists 1,500 Woodcutting and 1,500 Crafting;
content grants the dramen branch/staff and no XP.

Given that *seven* modern-only rewards have already been found correctly absent in quests
1–15, the most likely explanation is that these two are also modern additions. But "most
likely" is not verified, so these are recorded as **unresolved pending a 2004-era source**
rather than as defects, and nothing was changed.

The distinction matters: a false "gap" invites a fix that would *add* inauthentic content.
Leaving it unresolved is the safer error.
