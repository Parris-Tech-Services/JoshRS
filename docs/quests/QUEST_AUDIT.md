Generated by `tools/questops/audit-quests.mjs`. Do not hand-edit.

**Read this as triage, not a defect list.** Miniquests (e.g. Alfred Grimhand's Barcrawl)
legitimately have no quest points and no journal entry, so JOURNAL/REWARD gaps on those
are expected. Several quests also rely on the global varp registry in
`scripts/_unpack/<rev>/all.varp` instead of a local `.varp`.

This checks that a quest is **structurally capable** of working. It says nothing about
whether the dialogue matches the wiki, or whether the quest plays correctly — those need a
human and a real playthrough. A quest missing a flag below is definitely broken; a quest
with every flag is only *not obviously broken*.
