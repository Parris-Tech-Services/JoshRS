How to play

Move with WASD, arrow keys, the touch pad or tap the map. Tap a tree, rock, fishing spot or creature to walk over and interact.

Use E for a nearby resource, F to light logs, and H to eat cooked fish. Run mode increases movement speed.

Use the Town tab beside Banker Fern and Merchant Mira to store items, trade and complete repeatable deliveries. Town buttons activate when you stand beside the correct person.

Move your save between devices

Progress saves only in this browser. Download a backup, then import it on another device. Import replaces that device's progress after confirmation.

Welcome to JoshRS

Choose your path: tap a tree to harvest logs, find rocks for mining, catch fish by the western lake, and light a fire to cook your catch.

On mobile, tap the world to explore or use the directional buttons. On desktop, use arrows or WASD and press E, F or H for actions.
