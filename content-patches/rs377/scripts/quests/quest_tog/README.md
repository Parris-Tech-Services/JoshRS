# Tears of Guthix (9 May 2005)

JoshRS-original reconstruction. Start: Juna in the Lumbridge Swamp Caves. Chain: Juna gives an
unlit sapphire lantern -> light it with a tinderbox -> return to Juna. Rewards: 1 QP, access
to the tears cave (flavour only).

Known gaps: the stone bowl carving, the chasm climb with light creatures, and the weekly
tears minigame are not implemented. Stage values are JoshRS-original.
