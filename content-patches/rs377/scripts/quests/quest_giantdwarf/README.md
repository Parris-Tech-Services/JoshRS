# The Giant Dwarf (31 May 2005)

JoshRS-original reconstruction. Start: the boatman at the Keldagrim mines crossing.
Chain: boatman -> Black Guard leader -> Blue Opal director (6 gold ores) -> Black Guard
leader -> Supreme Commander. Rewards: 2 QP, 2,500 Crafting XP, 5,000 coins.

Known gaps: the boat cutscene, the eight-company choice, the trading floors, the library and
the statue reveal scene are not implemented. Stage values are JoshRS-original.
