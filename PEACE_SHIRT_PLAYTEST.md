# The Peace Shirt — human playtest

Prepared 13 November 2026 from the merged quest source. This checklist does not certify installation or Java-client gameplay. Claude owns installation and restart; wait for its ready message before testing the quest.

## Test now: prepare the wool

- [ ] Obtain three sheep fleeces (raw wool) through normal gameplay.
- [ ] Use one raw wool on a spinning wheel. Confirm one ball of wool replaces it.
- [ ] Repeat twice. The installed script processes one fleece per interaction.
- [ ] Keep the three balls for Ileana; do not hand them to Fred or Ned.

If shearing, finding a wheel, or spinning fails, record the location, clicked option and exact message. The wool-to-ball interaction is confirmed in installed source, but has not been verified in the Java client by this session.

## After Claude says the world is ready

Bring three balls of wool and leave backpack space for coins. Record your current coins, Crafting XP and Quest Points before the final conversation. This quest is in Burgh de Rott and has no Quest List entry. NPC conversations provide progress reminders.

The knitted row and shirt are represented by dialogue and quest progress: do not expect a new wearable shirt in your backpack. After completion Radu's Florin topic changes; his initial greeting may still be a normal greeting.

## Optional checks during this first playthrough

- Before accepting, decline once: no quest start should occur; you can ask again.
- Before bringing all three balls, talk to Ileana with zero or two: she should keep nothing. Return with all three to continue.
- At Florin's persuasion choice, try either wrong answer: he should return to the choice rather than advance or trap the conversation.
- Log out normally after an intermediate step: the next NPC should still recognise that step after login.
- If two people play, each should accept separately and bring their own three balls. One person's completion should not advance the other.
